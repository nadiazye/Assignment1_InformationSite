Origins

Spider-Man, also known as Peter Parker, is a beloved character that was created by Stan Lee and Steve Ditko in the year 1962 for Marvel Comics. After the success of the Fantastic Four, Stan Lee was looking for a new superhero idea. He has said that the teenage demand for comic books and a character with whom they could relate to led to the idea of Spider-Man. Spider-Man made his first appearance in the comic "Amazing Fantasy #15" in August 1962. Widely regarded as one of the most popular and commercially successful superheroes, he has been featured in comic books, television shows, films, video games, novels, and plays. Spider-Man's actual name is Peter Benjamin Parker. He's a kid from Queens, New York City, who went to live with his uncle and aunt, Ben and May Parker, after his parents passed away in an accident. Depending on the universe, (comic, movie, show, game, etc.) the location of which this occurred changes, but ultimately, Peter Parker became Spider-Man once he got bit by a radioactive spider.

Shortly after getting his powers, Parker starts to experiment with them, he makes a red and blue costume and signs up for a wrestling match. He wore a mask, the costume, and some self-made web spinners. After easily defeating the wrestler, a TV producer comes up to him for an interview and this is where Parker dons the name "Spider-Man". Shortly after his television spectactle, Peter purposely negelcted to stop an escaping thief, claiming that it was not his responsibility to do so. Peter forgot this incident as his fame rose, but one night he returned home to discover that his Uncle Ben had been murdered while trying to stop a burglar. Learning that the police had the burglar responsible holed up in a warehouse, Peter donned in his Spider-Man costume and went to avenge his uncle. After cornering and unmasking the criminal, Peter found that it was the same man who he let escape before. After taking out the criminal and giving him to police, Peter returned home and thought about how he could've stopped the man when he had the chance.

Filled with remorse, he realized that with great power, there must also come great responsibility. Peter eventually found himself consumed by the concept of adventure and heroism. That, along with the lesson in responsibility that was recently drilled into his head, inspired him to take that fateful step into the shoes of heroism, as the Amazing Spider-Man, New York's newest street level hero.

Spider-Man's Usual Enemies

Green Goblin

Norman Osborn, also known as the Green Goblin, is the co-founder and former CEO of Oscorp. Depending on the universe, Norman tends to come into contact with a chemical formula that drives him insane and leads him to become the supervillain Green Goblin. He seeks to ruin Spider-Man's life and become the ruler of New York's criminal underworld. The Green Goblin has battled Spider-Man multiple times over the years and has become Spider-Man's greatest enemy. Eventually, after being cleansed of his evil, or having the insanity somewhat at bay, Norman becomes good and seeks out repetance for his crimes. Norman has grappled with many things after his purification, constantly lying between the lines of achieving redemption and relapsing back into the Green Goblin identity.

Doctor Octopus

Doctor Otto Octavius, better known as Doc Ock or Doctor Octopus, is one of Spider-Man's greatest and most iconic enemies. Dr. Octavius was a great researcher and scientist. He delved himself deep into his studies due to previous losses and grief. His refusal to follow operational procedure led to a radiation leak during one of his experiments, which caused an explosion. Otto survived and discovered that this explosion had fused his mechanical arms to his torso, and that he was now able to telepatheically control his new arms. Recognizing this newfound ability gave him immense power, he held hospital staff that were treating his injuries hostage. This drew Spider-Man's attention, but with Octo's abilities and the strength of his arms, he defeated Spider-Man. With the arrogance of this victory, he then took control of a nuclear research facility. he was then forced to once again face Spider-Man, this time the wall-crawler won with a single punch to the jaw.

Lizard

Doctor Curtis Connors is a long time friend and ally of Spider-Man, while his alter ego the Lizard is a long time enemy. Curtis "Curt" Connors was born in Coral Gables, Florida. He was a gifted surgeon who enlisted in the U.S. Army and was sent off to war. He performed emergency battlefield surgery on wounded GIs, but his right arm was injured and had to be amputated. Connors returned to civilian life as a research technologist, and became obsessed with uncovering the secrets of reptilian limb regenration. Connors was working at his home in the Florida Everglades, he finally developed an experimental serum taken from reptilian DNA. He successfully regrew the missing limb of a rabbit and then chose to test on himself. Connors put the serum onto his missing limb, resulting into growing back his arm, but the formula had a side effect; Connors was subsequently transformed into a reptilian humanod monster. With the help of Connors' notes, Spider-Man was able to create an antedote to restore him to his human form.
