Origins

Spider-Man, also known as Peter Parker, is a beloved character that was created by Stan Lee and Steve Ditko in the year 1962 for Marvel Comics. After the success of the Fantastic Four, Stan Lee was looking for a new superhero idea. He has said that the teenage demand for comic books and a character with whom they could relate to led to the idea of Spider-Man. Spider-Man made his first appearance in the comic "Amazing Fantasy #15" in August 1962. Widely regarded as one of the most popular and commercially successful superheroes, he has been featured in comic books, television shows, films, video games, novels, and plays. Spider-Man's actual name is Peter Benjamin Parker. He's a kid from Queens, New York City, who went to live with his uncle and aunt, Ben and May Parker, after his parents passed away in an accident. Depending on the universe, (comic, movie, show, game, etc.) the location of which this occurred changes, but ultimately, Peter Parker became Spider-Man once he got bit by a radioactive spider.

Shortly after getting his powers, Parker starts to experiment with them, he makes a red and blue costume and signs up for a wrestling match. He wore a mask, the costume, and some self-made web spinners. After easily defeating the wrestler, a TV producer comes up to him for an interview and this is where Parker dons the name "Spider-Man". Shortly after his television spectactle, Peter purposely negelcted to stop an escaping thief, claiming that it was not his responsibility to do so. Peter forgot this incident as his fame rose, but one night he returned home to discover that his Uncle Ben had been murdered while trying to stop a burglar. Learning that the police had the burglar responsible holed up in a warehouse, Peter donned in his Spider-Man costume and went to avenge his uncle. After cornering and unmasking the criminal, Peter found that it was the same man who he let escape before. After taking out the criminal and giving him to police, Peter returned home and thought about how he could've stopped the man when he had the chance.

Filled with remorse, he realized that with great power, there must also come great responsibility. Peter eventually found himself consumed by the concept of adventure and heroism. That, along with the lesson in responsibility that was recently drilled into his head, inspired him to take that fateful step into the shoes of heroism, as the Amazing Spider-Man, New York's newest street level hero.

Spider-Man's Usual Enemies

(Based off Earth-616's Spider-Man)

Green Goblin

Norman Osborn, also known as the Green Goblin, is the co-founder and former CEO of Oscorp. Depending on the universe, Norman tends to come into contact with a chemical formula that drives him insane and leads him to become the supervillain Green Goblin. He seeks to ruin Spider-Man's life and become the ruler of New York's criminal underworld. The Green Goblin has battled Spider-Man multiple times over the years and has become Spider-Man's greatest enemy. Eventually, after being cleansed of his evil, or having the insanity somewhat at bay, Norman becomes good and seeks out repetance for his crimes. Norman has grappled with many things after his purification, constantly lying between the lines of achieving redemption and relapsing back into the Green Goblin identity.

Doctor Octopus

Doctor Otto Octavius, better known as Doc Ock or Doctor Octopus, is one of Spider-Man's greatest and most iconic enemies. Dr. Octavius was a great researcher and scientist. He delved himself deep into his studies due to previous losses and grief. His refusal to follow operational procedure led to a radiation leak during one of his experiments, which caused an explosion. Otto survived and discovered that this explosion had fused his mechanical arms to his torso, and that he was now able to telepatheically control his new arms. Recognizing this newfound ability gave him immense power, he held hospital staff that were treating his injuries hostage. This drew Spider-Man's attention, but with Octo's abilities and the strength of his arms, he defeated Spider-Man. With the arrogance of this victory, he then took control of a nuclear research facility. he was then forced to once again face Spider-Man, this time the wall-crawler won with a single punch to the jaw.

Lizard

Doctor Curtis Connors is a long time friend and ally of Spider-Man, while his alter ego the Lizard is a long time enemy. Curtis "Curt" Connors was born in Coral Gables, Florida. He was a gifted surgeon who enlisted in the U.S. Army and was sent off to war. He performed emergency battlefield surgery on wounded GIs, but his right arm was injured and had to be amputated. Connors returned to civilian life as a research technologist, and became obsessed with uncovering the secrets of reptilian limb regenration. Connors was working at his home in the Florida Everglades, he finally developed an experimental serum taken from reptilian DNA. He successfully regrew the missing limb of a rabbit and then chose to test on himself. Connors put the serum onto his missing limb, resulting into growing back his arm, but the formula had a side effect; Connors was subsequently transformed into a reptilian humanod monster. With the help of Connors' notes, Spider-Man was able to create an antedote to restore him to his human form.

Sandman

Sandman, actual name being William Baker, was born and raised in a rough neighborhood in New York City. After his tough upbringing, he partook in the art of sand sculptures. As an adult, he incurs a large debt to a mob which leads him to partake in illegal activity. His illegal activity increases in depth and scope, turning him into a violent and bitter man. Eventually he ends up in prison on Ryker's Island, where he escaped and fleed from into a nuclear testing site. This nuclear site was on a beach near Savannah, georgia where he comes itno contact with sand that had been irraditated by an experimental reactor. His body and the radioactive sand bonded, changing his molecular stricture into sand. He was impressed and proceeded to name himself the Sandman after his powers. Similar to the Green Goblin/ Norman Osborn, Sandman is another Spider-Man villain that struggles with his own morality.

Venom

Eddie Brock, also known as Venom, is the main host of an alien symbiote. Eddie Brock was originally a normal human and man, he was a rival photographer of Peter Parker's at the Daily Bugle. Eddie Brock has always treaded the line between hero and villain, sometimes looking to destory Spider-Man, and other times working with him. His relationship with the alien symbiote began after Eddie learned about his impending death, to which he decided to commit suicide to beat. As he was visiting Our Lady of Saints Church to beg forgiveness from God for his mortal sins, Spider-Man was using the church's bells to battle and take off his symbiotic alien costume. As the symbiote detached from Spider-Man due to the vibrations and sound, it trickled down and attached itself to Brock. It fed on his despair, adrenaline, and fierce hatred. The symbiote merged mentally and physically with Brock, whose bubbling hatred overwhelmed with the compassion the symbiote acquired from Spider-Man. The dominant thought for both organisms was that of Spider-Man, but Brock's sheer hatred for the web-slinger had twisted the symbiote's feelings. Brock soon discovered that the costume had given him all of Spider-man's powers as well as others. It also added to his already large fram and incresed his trength to superhuman levels. Brock and the symbiote then donned the name "Venom" and plotted their revenge. Through the symbiote, Brock learned about Spider-Man's secret identity, aiding in the hunt.

Carnage

Cletus Kasady is an unstable mass murdering serial killer. Cletus has fought many superheoes over th years, but considers Venom and Spider-Man to be his mortal enemies. Cletus is a deeply disturbed individial with a dark past. As a child, he killed his grandmother, killed a dog, and tried to kill his mother in a bathtub. With his psychotic and antisocial behavior, he was sent to a group home for boys where his behavior made him the target of bullying and torment. Eventually Kasady grew up to become a serial killer and commit 11 confirmed murders. Eventually Kasady was captured and sent to Ryker's Island. This is where he met and shared a cell with Eddie Brock. While Eddie waited for the symbiote to free him from prison, Kasady spoke to him about his homicidal philosophy on life: that life was essentially meaningless and futile, that "laws are only words", and came to see the spreading of chaos through random, unpatterned bloodshed as "the ultimate freedom." As anticipated, the symbiote broke Eddie out of prison, doing so by inciting a prison riot. Unbeknown to Eddie, the alien host was pregant and through this chaos, the symbiote alien gave birth. The symbiote's spawn ended up being left behind and was bonded to Cletus Kasady, mixing with Kasady's blood. Empowered beyond the levels of Spider-Man and Venom, Carnage made his escape-- with his new power, he was determined to disseminate his lethal philosophy.

Scorpion

MacDonald Gargan was originally a private investigator intially hired by J. Jonah Jameson to find out how Peter Parker was able to obtain his incredible photos of Spider-Man. Jameson later convinced him to undergo a procedure that granted him all the powers of a scorpion, but at the cost of his sanity. Thus, the creation of Scorpion, who proved to be more than a match for the spider. He defeated him twice, but the mutagenic treatment seriously affected gargan's mind, driving him insnae. He turned on Jameson, but Spider-Man was able to defeat the Scorpion for their third battle and save Jameson. Jameson was struck with the irony that he was saved by the man that he had originally tried to destory. Scorpion became a professional criminal and would return to indulge in his vendetta against Spider-Man and Jameson, only to be defeated again.

Rhino

Alexsei Sytsevich, better known as the Rhino. He volunteers to be bonded to an experimental exoskeleton by Norman Osborn, Rhino is empowered and is directed to kidnap Colonel John Jasmeson. However, the Rhino betrays Norman and goes on a rampage, eventually being defeated by Spider-Man. He is eventually approached by Norman again, agreeing to find and kidnap Bruce Banner (the alter ego of the Hulk). Banner becomes the Hulk and battles the Rhino, Rhino is caught in an explosion and is left in a coma.

Vulture

Adrian Toomes, also known as the Vulture, is one of Spider-Man's oldest villians. He is one of the first villians that Spider-Man battled in his career of heroism. As an adult, Toomes co-founded a company with Gregory Bestmen. The company was called Bestman and Toomes Electronics and had Toomes making inventions while Gregory saw to any and all administrative affairs. Toomes spent a large amount of time on a side project, an electromagnetic winged harness that allows the person who wears it to fly. However, one day when the harness was completed, Toomes went to Gregory to tell him the news and finds out that Gregory was embezzling funds from the company. Phsyically confronting his business partner, he found out that the harness fives him superhuman strength. Gregory got away from him and eventually took full control fo the company, leaving Toomes with no options. toomes retired to Staten Island with his life savings, taking his harness with him and working on it in his spare time. Seeing the potential for revenge with his new found strength and ability to fly, Toomes named himself the Vulture and donned a green costume. Vulture tried to look for evidence against his old partner Gregory, after no success he then began to rob company plant's to continue looking for evidence.

Electro

Maxwell Dillon was born to Jonathon and Anita Dillon in Endicott, New York. His father was an accountant, but had difficulty keeping a job. Max and his family moved frequently due to this, when his father eventually abandoned the family. His mother, Anita, sunsequently became overprotective of Max, leading ot his inferiority complex. Years later, Max told his mother that he wished to become an electrical engineer. His mother, not wanting him to be angry if he failed, told him he wasn't smart enough to become one. He reluctantly settled as a lineman for an electric company. While repairing a power line, a freak lightning accident resulted in a mutagenic change in his nervous system, transforming him into a living electrical capacitor. His powers were intially weak, so he spent some time stealing electrical equipment from Stark Industries to charge himself. During this time, he was approached by Magneto, who considered him a potential recruit for his Brotherhood of Evil Mutants, claiming that Dillon's power rivaled his own, but Dillon refused. The next day Dillon confronted a small-time thug who he'd been borrowing money from to pay for the machinery he needed. When the thug drew a gun on him because he hadn't paid his debt, Max responded by shooting a blast of lightning through the thug's chest. This became the first time, Dillon ever killed anybody, the first footstep into his life of crime.

Shocker

Herman Schultz, better known as the Shocker, is a member of the Sinister Six and former member of the Thunderbolts. The Shocker is a gifted, and apparently self-taught, engineer who built his battle suit from scratch in a prison workshop. He is also the (self-proclaimed) world's best safe-cracker. The Shocker (outside of the norm of most Spider-Man villains), is just a simple theif and not a psychopath with burning hatred for Spider-Man. The Shocker always goes after money and prefers to be paid than take any lives.

Mysterio

Quentin Beck, also known as Mysterio, is an adversary of Spider-Man and professional illusionist. Beck dreamed of movie making ever since childhood. With a battered old movie camera, Beck made monsters out of clay and made a few non-stop action films which lasted a few minutes. In his teens, Beck got a job in a low budget monster-thriller and used the money he made to move to Hollywood to seek fame and fortune. s the years went by, Beck became a famous stuntman and later a special effects artist, designing props and costumes. He even appeared as an alien extra in a science fiction film, which was later revealed to be an evil scheme by a criminal known as the Tinkerer. Despite his success, Beck wanted more, he grew tired of working behind the scenes and wanted to be a professional actor and director. However he didn't have the face or talent to be a star and lacked the patience to be part of a production crew. After a joke from a friend, Beck decided to use his talents to become a superhero, so using his knowledge of special effects and gadgets, he created a costume identity for himself: Mysterio, the Master of Illusion. After seeing his soon to be enemy Spider-Man branded a menace in the newspapers, Beck knew he would gain a lot of publicity for Spidey's capture. However Spider-Man hadn't broken the law, so Beck dressed up as him using special effects and devices to make him indistinguishable from the real hero and committed various robberies, framing the webslinger. He then paid Daily Bugle publisher J. Jonah Jameson a visit and promised to take down Spider-Man once and for all in exchange for a high fee. His first battle with Spider-Man took place at the Brooklyn Bridge and with his special "powers" of smoke and acid, Mysterio easily defeated Spider-Man.However during a press conference with J.J.J at the Daily Bugle, Spidey in his true identity as Peter Parker slipped a Spider tracer in the folds of Mysterio's cloak and followed him to a massive TV studio building. During the battle, Spidey tricked Mysterio into confessing that he framed the webslinger which was recorded on a miniature tape recorder. After destroying Mysterio's sonar device that jammed his spider sense, Spidey eventually captured Mysterio and turned him over to the authorities. After finding out the truth about Mysterio, J.J.J was horrified and even more furious that he had been proved wrong about Spider-Man once again.

Kingpin

Wilson Fisk, better known as the Kingpin is one of the most feared and powerful crime lords in the world and is the current leader of the Hand. Wilson Fisk began his life as a poor child in New York City, bullied by his classmates due to his obesity. Fisk began training himself in physical combat, using his new-found strength to intimidate the bullies into joining his gang. He was eventually discovered by crime lord Don Rigoletto. Fisk became Don Rigoletto's bodyguard and right-hand man. Eventually, Fisk killed Don Rigoletto and took control of his criminal empire, immediately becoming one of the most powerful figures in New York's underworld. While the Kingpin enjoyed a long tenure in his new position, he had made enemies such as the Maggia crime syndicate and the terrorist group HYDRA. The two groups teamed together to oppose Fisk, causing him to flee to Japan. There, he started a spice business in order to regain his wealth. After earning enough money, Fisk returned to New York and started gang wars, in an attempt to bring down the Maggia. With the criminal world in chaos, Fisk was able to step in and take back control. Fisk attempted a coalition of the New York mobs after hearing that Spider-Man had apparently gone out of action, and ordered the abduction of J. Jonah Jameson, but was foiled by Spider-Man in his first confrontation with the web-slinger.

Kraven the Hunter

Sergei Kravinoff, better known as Kraven the Hunter, is a long time enemy of Spider-Man and the half-brother of the Chameleon. Kraven is typically portrayed as a renowned big-game hunter whose goal in life is to beat Spider-Man to prove himself as the world's greatest hunter. Though he is often overconfident in his own abilities, about which he likes to boast, he possesses a great sense of honor, and treats his adversaries as equals until proven otherwise. Because of this, Kraven has sometimes been depicted as an antihero and has teamed up with other heroes, including Spider-Man.

Hobgoblin

Roderick Kingsley is the original and current Hobgoblin and the former CEO of Kingsley Ltd. Kingsley was a fashion designer operating in New York City. He used questionable and ruthless business practices, Roderick developed a financial empire reaching far beyond the fashion industry. Victim to one of Kingsley's machinations, rival designer Narda Ravanna, under the guise of Belladonna, attacked him but was stopped by Spider-Man. Kingsley acquired the Green Goblin's original suit and equipment from a bank robber being chased by Spider-Man named George Hill. Hill had discovered one of the many hideouts Norman Osborn employed, while in the sewers. Kingsley made Hill load a van with the equipment, and transfer it from the hideout to another location, then disposed of him, detonating a bomb hidden inside the van. As the Hobgoblin, Roderick went on a blackmail binge meant to cripple his major competitors. Feeling deficient in lacking the physical power to protect himself, Roderick sought out Norman Osborn's original Green Goblin formula. However, he needed a "guinea pig" before he could test the formula on himself and found one in petty crook Arnold "Lefty" Donovan. Monitoring Donovan's progress in the fight against Spider-Man, a satisfied Kingsley had his test subject terminated.Modifying the formula to avoid the insanity-inducing side effects, Kingsley found that it gave him strength roughly equal that of Spider-Man's, perhaps surpassing that of the Green Goblin.
