Origins

Spider-Man, also known as Peter Parker, is a beloved character that was created by Stan Lee and Steve Ditko in the year 1962 for Marvel Comics. After the success of the Fantastic Four, Stan Lee was looking for a new superhero idea. He has said that the teenage demand for comic books and a character with whom they could relate to led to the idea of Spider-Man. Spider-Man made his first appearance in the comic "Amazing Fantasy #15" in August 1962.
